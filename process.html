FAQ

Questions about the process

How much of my time does this take?

Usually one hour a week for the session, plus time to act on the plan. We do the heavy lifting where we can.

Do I need a store already?

No. The Launch Programme starts from an idea or a product and gets you set up properly.

Can my team join the calls?

Yes. We encourage it, and every session is recorded.

What if I want to stop?

Ongoing work is month to month. Give us 30 days notice and you keep everything we built together.

Do you do the work or just advise?

Both. We advise, and we roll up our sleeves on setup, ads and systems when it helps.

How do we measure success?

We agree the numbers that matter in the first two weeks and review them every month.
