How we work

A clear process, start to scale

You always know what is happening, who is doing it and how it is affecting your sales.

  1. Discovery call

    A free 30 minute call about your products, your numbers and where you want the business to be in a year.

  2. Audit and plan

    We go through your store, ads and analytics, then hand you a clear plan with priorities and costs.

  3. Build and launch

    Design, development, campaigns and flows, built in short sprints with you seeing progress every week.

  4. Test and optimise

    We track every change against sales, keep what works and cut what does not.

  5. Scale

    Once the numbers hold, we put more fuel behind what is working and open new channels.

Questions

About the process

Which platforms do you work with?

Mostly Shopify, with WooCommerce for brands that need it. If you are on something else, we will tell you honestly whether moving is worth it.

I am just starting out. Can you help?

Yes. We help new brands pick products, set up a store properly and plan the launch, so you avoid the expensive early mistakes.

How long does a store build take?

Most builds take three to six weeks depending on the number of products and custom features.

Will I be able to update the store myself?

Yes. Every store comes with a handover session and simple guides, so you can add products and edit pages without us.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. You can choose a monthly growth plan, or come back to us whenever you need a hand.
