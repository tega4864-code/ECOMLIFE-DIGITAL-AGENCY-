The 10 things to set up before you launch an online store

A launch goes better when the boring parts are done properly. Here is the checklist we run with every founder before a store goes live.

AO
Abraham OyeniranFounder, EcomLife

Launch day is exciting. It is also when every shortcut you took during setup comes back to bite. These are the ten things we make sure are in place before any store goes live.

The foundations

  1. Your numbers. Know your product cost, shipping cost, fees and target margin for every product before you set a price.
  2. Payments. Test checkout end to end with real cards and the payment methods your customers actually use, including local options.
  3. Shipping rules. Clear rates, delivery times and a policy for failed deliveries.
  4. Legal pages. Terms, privacy, refunds and cookies, written for your business and your country.
  5. Tax. Make sure prices and invoices handle tax correctly for where you sell.

The growth engine

  1. Tracking. Analytics and ad pixels installed and tested, so you can see what is working from the first order.
  2. Email capture. A reason to sign up, and a welcome email that actually sends.
  3. Abandoned cart flow. One of the highest earning emails you will ever set up.
  4. Product pages that sell. Clear photos, benefits before features, sizing or usage help, and real reasons to buy now.
  5. A 90 day plan. What you will do in the first three months to bring in customers, and how you will know if it is working.

Why this matters

Every item on this list is cheaper to fix before launch than after. Broken tracking means a month of ad spend you cannot learn from. Missing legal pages can get your payment account frozen. A slow, confusing product page wastes every visitor you pay for.
