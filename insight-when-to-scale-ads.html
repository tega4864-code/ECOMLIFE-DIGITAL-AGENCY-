How to know when it is time to scale your ad spend

Doubling your budget will not double your profit unless the business underneath is ready. Here are the signs it is, and the signs it is not.

AO
Abraham OyeniranFounder, EcomLife

Scaling ads is the moment many online businesses either take off or burn through their cash. The difference is rarely the ads themselves. It is whether the rest of the business can turn more traffic into more profit.

Signs you are ready to scale

  • You know your contribution margin per order and it is healthy after ad costs
  • Your conversion rate has been stable for at least a month
  • You have stock, or a reliable supplier, for at least twice your current volume
  • A good share of customers come back to buy again
  • Customer service and fulfilment are not already stretched

Signs you should fix things first

  • You are profitable on paper but cash is always tight
  • Returns or complaints are rising
  • Your best performing ads only work with heavy discounts
  • You are the only person who can pack, reply and post

How to scale without losing control

Increase budgets gradually, around 20 percent at a time, and give each change a few days before judging it. Watch profit per order, not just ROAS. Keep testing new creative, because the ads that work today will tire. And make sure your email and retention engine is running, so the customers you pay to acquire come back for free.
